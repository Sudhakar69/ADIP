How the interview actually runs

Real-life scenario interviews rarely ask "what is X." They hand you a broken system and watch how you reason. This board mirrors that shape everywhere.

01 — the page

You're told something is on fire: pods restarting, a pipeline failing, an ExpressRoute circuit down. That's your ticket header in this board.

02 — triage

You narrate the commands and dashboards you'd check first, in order. Every ticket's Diagnose section models this.

03 — resolution

You state the fix and why it's safe. The Resolution box is the answer an interviewer wants to hear.

04 — prevent recurrence

Senior candidates close with prevention: alerts, guardrails, IaC changes. The Pitfall box flags what juniors skip.
